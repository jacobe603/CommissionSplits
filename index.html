Commission Split Engine - How It Works

1. Executive Summary

This feature implements a "Waterfall" calculation engine to distribute profit on HVAC sales projects. The logic dictates how a project's commission pool is divided among four potential parties: the Owner, the Specialist, the Engineering Team, and the Contractor Team.

Core Logic ("The Waterfall")

The calculation follows a strict order of operations:

  • Top-Off Deductions: The Owner and Specialist (if involved) take their percentage cuts from the total project profit first.
  • The Split: The remaining profit is divided between the Engineering Team and the Contractor Team based on a user-defined "Influence Level" (e.g., Flat Spec vs. Open Spec).
  • Inside Sales Costs: From those two team buckets, we deduct fixed percentages to pay the Inside Sales/Project Management staff.
  • Net Commission: The remaining amount in the buckets is distributed to the Outside Sales Reps based on a pre-defined matrix.

Note: If a Specialist is involved, they "cannibalize" a portion of the Inside Sales budget (effectively re-allocating it to the Specialist Support role).

3. Algorithm Logic

The calculation must be performed in this specific order:

Step 1: Determine Inside Sales Rates

If a specialist is active, the standard inside sales rates are reduced by 2%, and that amount is re-allocated to the Specialist Support role.

IF specialist_active:
    eng_inside_rate = 7% - 2% = 5%
    cont_inside_rate = 5% - 2% = 3%
    spec_inside_rate = 2% + 2% = 4%
ELSE:
    eng_inside_rate = 7%
    cont_inside_rate = 5%

Step 2: Calculate Remaining Pool

Deduct the Owner and Specialist Outside percentages from the total (100%).

remaining_pool = 100% - owner% - specialist%

Step 3: Divide the Pool

IF direct_sale:
    eng_gross = 0%
    cont_gross = remaining_pool
ELSE:
    eng_gross = remaining_pool × influence_factor
    cont_gross = remaining_pool - eng_gross

Step 4: Validation ("Squeeze" Check)

Verify that the gross pools are large enough to cover the mandatory Inside Costs. If not, an error is displayed.

Step 5: Net Commission

eng_outside_net = eng_gross - eng_inside_rate
cont_outside_net = cont_gross - cont_inside_rate
